# Governance

Food Help maintains generic directory software. Each deployment operator owns local inclusion criteria, evidence review, publication decisions, correction handling, data rights, optional analytics policy and hosting approvals.

The root `AGENTS.md` defines platform boundaries. A narrower `site/AGENTS.md` may add local review and operational rules, but may not turn generated files into authority or introduce community-specific platform logic. Keep private research notes and credentials outside the public repository.

The authority order is explicit user/owner direction within the task, applicable repository instructions, authoritative site facts/configuration and schema/domain contracts. Generated reports demonstrate a build; they do not override reviewed source facts or authorize external actions.

Ordinary code fixes can follow normal maintainer review. Publication of provider facts, licence changes, enabling collection, deployment/DNS/domain actions and public repository creation need the appropriate operator/owner approval. A passing check is necessary engineering evidence, not that approval.

Before a consequential change, hand off its exact scope, source/data difference, public and privacy effects, cache/update implications, test results, remaining limitations, artifact hash and proposed external action. Keep this in the relevant review or release record. Do not create a parallel historical “source of truth” archive inside generic source.
