# Governance

Food Help maintains generic directory software. Each deployment operator owns local inclusion criteria, evidence review, publication decisions, correction handling, data rights, optional analytics policy and hosting approvals.

The root `AGENTS.md` defines platform boundaries. A narrower `deployments/<community>/AGENTS.md` may add local review and operational rules, but may not turn generated files into authority or introduce community-specific platform logic. A self-hosted configuration outside `deployments/` can carry the same narrower instruction file beside its `site.json`. Keep private research notes and credentials outside the public repository.

The authority order is explicit user/owner direction within the task, applicable repository instructions, authoritative site facts/configuration and schema/domain contracts. Generated reports demonstrate a build; they do not override reviewed source facts or authorize external actions.

Ordinary code fixes can follow normal maintainer review. Publication of provider facts, licence changes, enabling collection, deployment/DNS/domain actions and public repository creation need the appropriate operator/owner approval. A passing check is necessary engineering evidence, not that approval.

Before a consequential change, hand off its exact scope, source/data difference, public and privacy effects, cache/update implications, test results, remaining limitations, artifact hash and proposed external action. Keep this in the relevant review or release record. Do not create a parallel historical “source of truth” archive inside generic source.

`main` contains shared software and independently selectable community folders. A community release identifies one exact commit and one target. Merging a shared change or adding a folder is not approval to publish all communities. Keep a per-community release/rollback record without storing account receipts or credentials in public source. The historical `kingston` branch is retained for recovery, not ongoing development after the new release arrangement is verified.

Contributors retain their rights under the applicable licences. Governance does not require a copyright assignment or imply that True Good Craft owns every contribution. Data grants, third-party notices and trademark rights remain separate; see [NOTICE](NOTICE.md).
